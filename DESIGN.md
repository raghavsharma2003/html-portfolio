# Vyakti personal studio, replacement direction 2026-09-27

## Commitment

A personal creative workbench for an expert teaching their AI. The product is
human and calm: pearl white working paper, graphite type, metallic edges,
small indigo selection accents. Instrument Sans is the existing local family.
No giant dials, technical dashboard chrome, drifting text, or decorative meters.
The owner has rejected the previous interface; it is an anti-reference.

## Layout and journey

A consistent workspace frame remains through building, testing and sharing.
Desktop: a narrow navigation column, identity at top, one generous content area.
Phone: concise horizontal section choices and a bottom navigation with four
primary destinations. Knowledge, voice and personality are discoverable before
voice is ready. Live conversation, private rehearsal and publishing retain their
real server eligibility; visual navigation does not imply capability approval.

The overview gives concrete setup actions and honest saved state. Forms have one
primary action and inline recovery. Existing consent is recorded once for its
actual scope; no silent expansion of a permission. Data retention and scope are
separate from design decoration. No invented completion percentage or quality score.

## Material, type and motion

Paper #f7f8fa, surface #ffffff, ink #20242d, secondary #5a6473,
line #dce1e8, active #4452bd. Body 15px, controls14-15px, headings28-36px.
Use one elevation treatment per region. Images belong to identity/entry and
empty context, never behind input controls. One authored tab-selection movement
and a short content crossfade connect states. Reduced motion removes both.

Stable flow layout, no absolute full-viewport scenes below fixed overlays.
44px targets, visible focus, body contrast4.5:1, text200% and360px reflow.
Any active recording/local sample locks navigation until kept or discarded.
An upload failure keeps the same sample available for retry.

## Design derivation

Considered editorial desk, recording console, teaching workbook, address book,
archive gallery, professional workbench and annotated library. The design seed
assigned the sixth direction. The professional workbench carries the owner's
clean premium material commitment and everyday work scene. Barcode fields,
flight instruments and developer-console challengers lose product clarity and
nontechnical audience fit; retain their discipline of stable states and precise
alignment, not their motifs. Surreal/weather motifs do not belong in controls.
