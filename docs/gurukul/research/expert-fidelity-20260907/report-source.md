# Vyakti expert fidelity and product direction

Prepared for the founder by Codex. Evidence reviewed 6–7 September 2026. This is a research and implementation brief, not a launch or quality certification.

## The product we should prove

An expert gives Vyakti their knowledge and real examples of how they explain, decide and speak. They meet their AI, correct it and approve changes. Their clients then get useful help between human sessions, with continuity specific to each client and a clear route back to the expert.

The first audience should be Hindi and Hinglish educators or coaches who already have paying cohorts and repeated questions. This is a proposed focus, not established product–market fit. Their existing audience provides a practical distribution path; the paid outcome is faster learner progress and less repeated expert work.

The product flow is Feed it → Meet it → Deploy it. Sign in and complete the necessary permissions once in the setup flow, add knowledge or record examples, then try realistic client questions. Correction is the central interaction: demonstrate what is wrong, compare the revision, approve it, and retain an undo path. Publishing should use the existing Rooms system.

Faithfulness has four separate tests: decisions, voice and delivery, memory and relationships, and listening. An eloquent response does not prove any of them. We can model observable behavior and owner-selected expression; we cannot substantiate copied consciousness, inner feelings or an exact human duplicate.

## What exists and what remains unproven

| Layer | Existing foundation | Important remaining gap |
|---|---|---|
| Knowledge and mind | Sources, interviews, claims, owner decisions and materialization | Demonstrate correction transfer to unseen questions |
| Voice | Chatterbox, IndicF5, Qwen3-TTS and VoxCPM evaluation services | Owner-accepted Hindi, Hinglish and English likeness |
| Relationships | Person-by-agent facts and structural disclosure rules | Relevant older memory can lose to eight recent facts in Mirror |
| Expression | Source-bound observations and an experience compiler | Capture exists; a product reader and approved delivery control are incomplete |
| Teaching the AI | Person Model review, creator correction queue, Mirror feedback | Written feedback transport repaired; recorded correction lineage remains incomplete |
| Sharing | Creator publication and client Rooms | Generalize teacher-specific assumptions without duplicating publication |

These findings come from caller tracing in the integration worktree. A defined function is not evidence of a connected feature. GroupAI contributes a narrow handoff/disclosure path; the audited branch does not establish a fully connected universal runtime. There is no need to invent another Human OS beside the existing compiler and relational engine.

The integration preserves the latest completion branch and the original checkout’s additional voice work. Its development database is isolated from production. Authentication, bounded replica operations and selected real SQL checks have run; the entire release gate and the complete voice journey have not been accepted.

## Which models we actually have

| Model | Local evidence | Research implication |
|---|---|---|
| Chatterbox Multilingual | Existing reference cloning and a historical owner LoRA experiment | Reproduce adapter-on versus adapter-off with exact artifact receipts |
| IndicF5 | Pretrained reference audio and transcript conditioning | Strong Hindi candidate; its listed languages do not establish English or Hinglish quality |
| Qwen3-TTS Base | Pretrained reference cloning, currently English evaluation | Official ten-language list excludes Hindi; do not call it our Hindi fine-tune |
| VoxCPM2 | Existing dual-reference conditioning already implemented | Test matched references before adding a redundant new mechanism |
| OmniVoice | Research challenger, not integrated or locally evaluated | Consider a bounded comparison after existing baselines are reproducible |

The real historical Chatterbox training used one speaker and 71 seconds of source audio for 60 epochs. ECAPA mean changed from 0.775278 to 0.795857 in two measurement runs per arm. No held-out split or blinded owner acceptance establishes a quality win. Azure confirms a past successful training job; a mutable image tag and optional adapter support do not prove ordinary previews use that checkpoint. See the repository’s Azure voice provenance report.

## How we test a faithful expert

**Voice and delivery.** Proposed first battery: 60 held-out utterances, 20 each in Hindi, Hinglish and Indian English, with three synthesis seeds per candidate. Compare matched genuine owner references and identical text. The owner and five bilingual listeners rate blinded pairs for identity, pronunciation, naturalness, accent and code-switch seams. Analyze by utterance, not as if three seeds were three independent speakers. Keep existing sealed listening protocols intact; version any expanded protocol separately.

Start with existing Chatterbox and VoxCPM settings and the IndicF5 route. Compare reference selection, normalization and whole-utterance versus semantic-clause synthesis before spending on more training. A proposed promotion threshold is over 60% paired preference with an uncertainty interval excluding chance, owner acceptance, and no language-specific or critical semantic regression. This is a decision rule to test, not an achieved result. Embedding similarity remains secondary.

**Decisions and corrections.** Use 20 expert-provided corrections spanning facts, explanation order, pronunciation, uncertainty and interpersonal boundaries. Test the corrected example and a new related case. Also test an unrelated case for regression. Every saved correction needs a receipt; rejected or undone changes must stop influencing behavior. A positive rating alone must not silently rewrite the expert.

Research on 1,052 American participants found combined interview and survey agents reached 86% of participants’ own retest consistency on held-out survey items. That supports careful elicitation and a human-consistency baseline, not an “86% human clone” claim or proof for Indian expert conversation. [Park and colleagues, revised 28 June 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.10109v3).

**Memory and relationships.** Evaluate 40 cases covering retrieval, updates, temporal reasoning, multiple sessions and abstention, with cross-person negatives. Current Mirror recall selects at most eight approved facts by priority and recency; it does not use the current question. An old commitment can therefore disappear behind recent unrelated facts. Compare query-relevant selection only after reproducing this baseline and preserving exact audience and retraction rules. The evaluation shape follows [LongMemEval, ICLR 2025, cleaned histories September 2025](https://github.com/xiaowu0162/LongMemEval).

**Listening and expression.** Test pauses inside sentences, self-repair, genuine endings and interruptions on phone speakers and headsets. Measure cut-off words, false endings, interruption stop time and response gaps. The current expression observations include duration, script transitions and energy; these do not identify mood or capture pitch and emphasis. Owner-labeled examples should precede durable style control. Prosody can help predict turn-taking, but this research does not establish Hindi deployment readiness. [Ekstedt and Skantze, SIGDIAL 2022](https://aclanthology.org/2022.sigdial-1.51/).

## Why someone would pay

Voice and persona alone are already competitive features. Delphi lists voice chat, multilingual support and creator plans including Builder at $79 per month and Scaler at $299 per month. Coachvox sells expert-content AI with client workflows and paid access. Sensay now emphasizes preserving employee knowledge during offboarding. These are first-party product claims and prices, not independent evidence of customer retention or our willingness-to-pay.

Sources checked 6 September 2026: [Delphi pricing](https://www.delphi.ai/pricing), [Coachvox product](https://coachvox.ai/), [Coachvox paid access guide, 6 May 2026](https://support.coachvox.ai/article/72-how-to-get-paid-for-ai-coaching), [Sensay product](https://sensay.io/).

| Direction | Customer pain | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Expert cohort assistant | Repeated questions and stalled learners between sessions | Lead with a paid pilot |
| Coach continuity | Clients forget plans and need contextual follow-through | Adjacent pilot after a narrow domain works |
| Enterprise knowledge continuity | Departing staff take tacit knowledge with them | Later; different buyer and procurement cycle |
| Broad consumer replicas | Novelty and access to personalities | Defer until useful recurring behavior is proven |
| Voice infrastructure | Accent, identity and latency requirements | Keep as enabling technology until externally validated |

Proposed pilot: three to five experts with existing paid audiences, a measured baseline week and four weeks of use. Track correctly resolved questions, expert minutes saved after review, repeat weekly use, learner task completion, paid renewal and support burden. Interview dropouts. Continued usage and renewal matter more than conversation length. No pilot, paid demand or retention result has yet been recorded for this integration.

## Economics and the next build order

Charge for an expert workspace with included usage and explicit overage limits. Test client access pricing only where the expert has an existing paid offer. Avoid unlimited voice before measuring accepted-output cost. Pricing and pilot thresholds are hypotheses, not a revenue forecast or valuation.

Measure cost per successfully completed client task: model tokens + transcription + accepted voice + rejected synthesis and cold starts + storage + infrastructure + support and payment costs. GPU generation time is not the same as the user’s audio duration. Public provider latency on H100, H200 or RTX 4090 is not our protected T4 end-to-end latency.

For the verified Azure gpt-4.1-mini GlobalStandard deployment, the retrieved rates were $0.40 per million input tokens and $1.60 per million output tokens. A hypothetical 2,000-input, 400-output turn is $0.00144 for text only. Four actual development ledger receipts totaled $0.000152, from much smaller synthetic probes; neither number includes voice or establishes full product margin. [Azure retail prices API](https://prices.azure.com/api/retail/prices), filtered to eastus2 and gpt 4.1 mini on 6 September 2026; exact filter and evidence are retained in the local rate evidence file.

Build order: repair the feedback contract and release failures; reproduce the memory limitation; connect an approved correction to a demonstrably better next answer; reproduce the existing voice baselines with verified checkpoint adoption; run the blinded multilingual battery; then optimize protected first-audible latency and cost. Keep the creator’s Feed, Meet, Deploy journey as the organizing surface throughout.

Potential defensibility is the combination of permissioned expert examples, reliable correction transfer, contextual memory, language-specific quality evidence and distribution through expert workflows. Foundation models will keep improving. Replaceable model adapters and repeated held-out evaluation are a better response than assuming any one model or laboratory cannot catch up.

### Evidence gaps that change the decision

- Owner listening acceptance and repeated-speaker generalization are absent. No claim of exact duplication, frontier leadership or a thousand-fold quality gain is supported.
- Full enrollment and protected voice are not yet proven in the isolated integration environment. Development connectivity is a narrower result.
- The commercial pilot has not run. Pricing, margins, retention and valuation remain unproven.
- Expression capture must remain separate from an inferred psychological label. The expert controls durable changes; session expression need not become a permanent trait.
