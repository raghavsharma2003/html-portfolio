|---|---|
| Correct period | T = 45 / 18 = 2.5 seconds, with the unit attached. |
| Correct frequency | f = 18 / 45 = 0.4 Hz, or equivalent reciprocal calculation. |
| Correct misconception | Makes clear that 2.5 is the period in seconds, not frequency in Hz. |
| Teaches the distinction | Period is time per oscillation; frequency is oscillations per second. |
