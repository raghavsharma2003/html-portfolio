## Review corrections already incorporated

1. An ephemeral observed `private_text_epoch` is a transaction CAS, not part of the immutable publication snapshot. Private edits do not invalidate the link or adopt new teaching choices. Each dispatch persists its observed epoch; a Never-rule/authority edit after rules were read prevents old gated output from committing or replaying. Fresh questions can use the still-published projection under fresh authority.
2. Checking retired IDs before INSERT was insufficient against a deleted conflicting row during a lock wait. The unique claim-once ledger closes that race for both publication and request IDs. Provider namespace remains `text-publication:<request_id>` with no possible second admission incarnation.
3. Terminal mutations may see a newly committed row through UPSERT/row locking that a sibling CTE cannot see through its old statement snapshot. Unpublish, visitor forget, consent withdrawal and expiry have fresh exact-scope cleanup statements. New joins with no question are included in withdrawal cleanup. Expiry also retries closed publications with leftover admission/ciphertext after an interrupted cleanup.
4. Claim response loss after a committed dispatch can be terminalized without its token only after the exact bound spend row is `released`. Generic null-token modification of a live dispatch remains refused. An attempted provider begin stays uncertain, not released.
5. Missing/malformed expiry aggregates are named failure, never zero success. Required cron authentication configuration is a platform readiness condition. The scheduled caller, bounded drain and every-ten-minute registry entry are in the runtime agent's files; actual scheduler activation and observed deletion lag remain deployment proof.
