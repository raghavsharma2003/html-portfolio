# Expert product: checkpoint 25

7 September 2026. This checkpoint improves the private creator journey. Full release acceptance is pending at freeze.

## What changes for an expert

**First use:** account and consent reads now have distinct checking, retry and ready states. Confirmed creation opens Files first, with voice available as an explicit alternative. Late reads cannot erase newer consent receipts or change another signed-in account.

**Feed → Meet:** an eligible uploaded document has a direct **Test this source** action. Visiting your files preserves your unfinished question and selected teaching draft in memory. Returning checks the exact source again and asks you to confirm the selected material. It never sends a question automatically. Processed files with private phrase suggestions remain testable too.

**Edit your teaching style:** Save follows the editable choices. Longer read-only details sit in an expandable section. Invalid saved content remains visible as a warning and is preserved until an explicit edit. Publication still requires a separate review of the exact saved sheet. A delayed Load preserves newer edits; Save confirms only its submitted revision. Uncertain save outcomes offer explicit readback.

## What remains open

- An ordinary owner can test private text before verified voice. Public sharing still lacks a complete text publication permission and capability path.
- One fresh uploaded file now produced an actual Azure answer with correct 18-minute checking, then 25-minute graphing, 43-minute total and an honest unknown student count in Roman Hinglish. Cost: $0.000539; replay made no second call. Separate cleanup completed. This is one synthetic example, not a general answer-quality benchmark.
- Saved teaching guidance persists, but recent real responses did not reliably follow it. A 12-call representation diagnostic is exploratory: one English answer introduced a chronology error despite following the requested mention order.
- Owner voice likeness, Hindi/Hinglish listening quality, identity verification, a full authenticated owner browser journey, customer willingness to pay and superiority over competitors are not accepted results.
