## Key sourced numbers

- Vendor pricing India: Bolna ₹7/min→₹3 at volume (+$0.02/min platform); Sarvam ₹6/min AI calls, ₹1.5/min BYO-model; Gnani est. ₹22–35/min biometric flows; Haptik ₹15L–2.5Cr/yr enterprise; Exotel PSTN ₹0.40–1.20/min; insurance-sector loaded ₹20–30/min; real-estate ₹25–65/min.
- Insurance: lapse 25–30% → 8–12% with voice-AI renewal campaigns; prevented lapse worth ₹50k–5L lifetime premium; ICICI Lombard ~1M bot calls/mo (70% of inbound, up from 10%).
- Collections: Gnani Collect365 NBFC case ₹20 Cr/mo at 63% collection rate; ₹15–25 per outcome bucket 0-30; RBI bans contact-shaming, 8AM–7PM window, mandatory disclosures.
- AstroTalk: FY25 revenue ₹1,182 Cr, ~₹250 Cr profit, ₹5–200+/min consumer-paid, ~20% platform commission, 65% chat. THE willingness-to-pay comparable: Indians pay per-minute for memoryless listening.
- Elder-care: Emoha FY25 ₹68 Cr (+631% YoY), NRI-heavy customer base; Carissa ₹799/mo AI check-ins; typical NRI layered spend ₹4,000–10,000/mo.
- Spoken-English: Stimuler $5–7/mo, 45k paying users, $3.75M raise; ELSA ~$12/mo; India AI-tutor cluster ₹420–1,000/mo.
- Companion category: global $120M in 2025; 12-mo LTV/payer $30.16; monthly-plan 12-mo retention 6.1%; top 10% of apps take 89% of revenue; **paid Meta/Google channels largely closed to the category** — Telegram/creator/organic only. Indian entrants: Companion Labs ($2.5M seed), Flingo, Parivar AI, Meetra (no disclosed revenue).
- Wysa: 7M+ reach, consumer $74.99/yr, B2B ~$50/employee/yr — revenue mostly employer contracts.
- Edtech: counselors 80–120 calls/day; 3.2 vs optimal 8–12 touchpoints; 78% enroll with fastest responder; PhysicsWallah/Allen/Aakash scholarship-test funnels.

## Regulatory (live constraints)

- TRAI TCCCPR 2026: mandatory AI disclosure on calls, consent verification, 140-series (promo) / 1600-series (transactional) number segregation, penalties to ₹10L; full effect deadline 2026-03-10 (ALREADY PASSED — any outbound B2B pilot must be DLT-compliant day one).
- RBI digital-lending + IRDAI: regulated entity is liable for AI conduct including mis-selling; disclosure-at-open mandatory both regimes.
- DPDP Act full effect 2027-05-14: cross-session personal/emotional memory storage needs its OWN specific, unbundled consent; penalties to ₹250 Cr. Product implication: a dedicated memory-consent screen, not a ToS checkbox.
