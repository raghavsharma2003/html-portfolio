## Notes on method and gaps

- All figures above were checked against at least one search; where I could reach
  a primary source (company press release/blog, GlobeNewswire, a named regulator
  filing, or a major outlet with named on-the-record figures) I did, and noted
  where a claim rests only on secondary aggregators (electroiq, aicompanionpick,
  demandsage, etc.) — those sites frequently republish each other's numbers
  without clear original sourcing, so treat their unique claims (e.g. "$1.4B CHAI
  valuation trajectory," "70M CHAI ARR" for 2026) as **unverified** even though
  they're mentioned above for completeness.
- **I could not independently verify** CHAI's implied "funding round by AMD and
  CoreWeave" as a priced equity round — the primary source (CHAI's own release)
  never states a dollar amount, only that AMD/CoreWeave provided "compute and
  capital resources." Treat "CHAI funding round" language in any strategy doc as
  imprecise until a number surfaces.
- I did **not** find any primary Nasscom publication containing the $28B→$140.8B
  figure under Nasscom's own name — only a community-submitted post hosting a
  Grand View Research number. This should be corrected in future strategy docs.
- WebFetch access worked throughout this session (no access failures to report);
  one fetch (community.nasscom.in) returned HTTP 406 and had to be worked around
  via a second targeted search rather than a direct read — noted, not a gap in
  the underlying finding since the Grand View Research figure was independently
  confirmed elsewhere.
- I did not attempt to verify every secondary "AI companion market size by 2035"
  number beyond confirming the specific $28.19B/$140.75B pairing traces to Grand
  View Research — multiple research firms (Precedence, GVR, others) publish
  divergent long-range forecasts for the same category and none should be
  treated as more than an order-of-magnitude signal.
