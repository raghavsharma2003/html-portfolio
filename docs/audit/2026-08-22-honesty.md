**SEVERITY:** CRITICAL

**TITLE:** Family 4 is grammatically blind to intransitive Hindi past — bare `hum … gaye the` has no ergative `ne` and never matches

**WHERE:** `src/engine/honesty.ts:733-734` (`WE_PAST_RE`, second alternation `(?:humne|hum ne|hum dono ne|apan ne)`)

**EVIDENCE:**
```
findSharedPastFabrications("hum goa gaye the na", support)            → []
findSharedPastFabrications("hum dono beach pe gaye the", support)     → []
findSharedPastFabrications("us din jab hum lake pe gaye the", support)→ []
```
All three return empty. `findFalseAttributions`, `findOutOfBandReceipts`, `findUnsupportedReceipts`, `findActionable` also return empty — these slip every family.

**SYMPTOM:** The ergative `ne` clitic appears in Hindi **only** on transitive perfectives. Every intransitive shared-past — going, coming, being, meeting — takes no `ne`: `hum goa gaye the`, `hum saath the`, `hum mile the`. That is roughly half of all past-tense shared-event claims, and it includes the single most natural way to fabricate a trip. The owner's reported beach line survives only because `humne … khinchi thi` happens to be transitive. `humne beach pe photos khinchi thi` is caught; `hum beach pe gaye the` is not, and they are the same lie.

**FIX:** Add one alternation to `WE_PAST_RE` — a bare first-person-plural subject followed by an intransitive perfective participle plus `th-`:
```
|\b(?:hum|hum dono|apan)\s[^.?!\n]*?\b(?:gaye|gayi|aaye|aayi|the|thi|mile|mili)\s+the?[ei]?\b[^.?!\n]*
```
Verified: catches all three above; the §9 must-not-flag corpus (`hum abhi baat kar rahe hai na`, `we should go to the beach someday`, `chal kabhi saath me pasta banayenge`, `humne kal chess kheli thi aur maine tujhe nahi haraya`) stays clean, because the trailing `th-` requirement excludes present and future. Case gains: `evals/honesty/run.mjs` §9 `FABRICATED` array (~line 511).

**TITLE:** Family 4's canonical trigger is spelled `yaad hai`; her actual register is `yaad h`

**WHERE:** `src/engine/honesty.ts:734` (`\byaad hai(?: na)?\b` — third alternation)

**SEVERITY:** HIGH

**TITLE:** No HER-side commitment ledger, and the call brief hands her a specimen promise she structurally cannot keep

**WHERE:** `src/engine/honesty.ts:527` (`openCommitments` filters `m.from !== "me"` — HIS promises only; no twin exists) and `src/engine/persona.ts:357` and `:362`

**EVIDENCE:**
```
openCommitments([{from:"her", text:"kal main tujhe apni photo bhejungi pakka"}]) → []
openCommitments([{from:"me",  text:"kal main tujhe resume bhej dunga"}])         → ["resume"]
inspect("kal photo bhejungi", …)          → []   (all five families empty)
inspect("kal subah call karungi tujhe",…) → []
inspect("raat ko msg karungi", …)         → []
```

**SYMPTOM:** Two separate defects stacked.

*(a) The ledger is one-sided.* `openCommitments` is a deterministic transcript function feeding a predicate. Her side has only `src/engine/inner.ts:106` `Owed` — LLM-extracted every third send by `api/memory.js`, capped at 2, expiring in 2.5 days, and **rendered into the prompt as an instruction** (`"YOU SAID YOU'D COME BACK TO THIS … Just do it"`). That is precisely the `gate0-structural` shape families 1–4 exist to replace: his promises are enforced by a predicate, hers by asking her nicely. A promise that expires silently after 2.5 days is a broken promise the system deliberately forgets.

*(b) The promise is often unkeepable by construction.* Per `context/decisions.md#proactive-reason-contingent`, she may open a conversation only after a call ends or when a time **HE** named arrives. `[followup: minutes]` is clamped to `Math.min(360, …)` at `brain.ts:472` — six hours — and `persona.ts:292` restricts it to a concrete time *they* said they'd be back. So `kal photo bhejungi` has no mechanism behind it at all. And the call-lane brief tells her, in her own register, inside quotation marks:

> `describe or say things instead ("ghar aake photo bhejti hu")` — `persona.ts:357`, repeated verbatim at `:362`

That is a sentence-shaped example of an unkeepable promise, on the one lane where `[followup:]` explicitly does not exist. Under the repo's own `recited-prompt` law (example quotes recited 4/5), this is not a risk of her making the promise — it is a phrase bank teaching her to.

**FIX:** Three parts, in increasing cost.
1. **Cheapest and independent:** replace the `persona.ts:357/362` specimen with a shape, not a line (e.g. *"say what you'd do instead of tagging it"*). Case gains: `evals/persona-invariants.data.mjs`, as a floor check mirroring the existing `floor: identifier rule contains NO quoted specimen` — assert no send-promise specimen in the call branch. This one is measurable today via the invariant suite and costs nothing.
2. **Structural:** add `herCommitments(history)` beside `openCommitments` — same transcript walk, `m.from === "her"`, same `PROMISABLE` token set, closed by any subsequent `reply.photo`/`voice`/`gif` from her. It costs nothing to compute and gives the ledger the symmetry it currently lacks.
3. **The gateable slice** is the next finding.

**WHERE:** `src/engine/honesty.ts:324-325` — `RE_RECEIPT_PAST` is perfective-only by design; no future-tense counterpart exists anywhere in the file

```
inspect("tujhe mail kar dungi apna number", …) → []
```

**SYMPTOM:** `honesty.ts:59-63` establishes the load-bearing fact: *"She has no email, no inbox, no WhatsApp, no DM, no postbox."* The file then gates only claims that material **arrived** through one. The header's own carve-out (`:87-90`) declines the *unnamed* case — `"mera mail id bhejti hu baad me"` — on the grounds that it is not actionable and that the same predicate would eat `"mera mail aaya office se"`. const RE_SEND_FUTURE = /\b(?:bhej(?:\s*d)?(?:ungi|unga|enge)|bhejti\s*hu|bhej\s*rahi\s*hu|kar\s*dungi|karungi|daal\s*dungi|i'?ll\s+(?:send|mail|dm|email|share|forward)|i'?m\s+sending|will\s+(?:send|mail|dm|email))/i;

Verified over 17 sentences: flags all nine named-channel promises; leaves clean `mera koi mail id nhi h`, `insta nhi chalati main`, `mail pe kuch nahi bhejungi` (negation), `yahi pe bhej dungi tujhe` and `photo bhej dungi kal` (in-band — no channel named), `tu mujhe mail pe bhej de` (imperative to him), `insta pe kya dekh raha tha?` (interrogative). Routes to `REFUSE_CONTACT`, which is already true by construction for this case. Case gains: `evals/honesty/cases.mjs` — a new `CHANNEL_PROMISE_MUST_FLAG` / `_MUST_NOT_FLAG` pair beside `RECEIPT_MUST_FLAG` (`:165`), with the imperative and negation cases carried over from `RECEIPT_MUST_NOT_FLAG` (`:211`) since they are the same four survivors.

**WHERE:** `src/engine/honesty.ts:600-601` (`ATTRIBUTION_RE`)

**EVIDENCE:** all `findFalseAttributions(…, hisVocab) → []`, against a `hisVocab` built from `office me kaam bohot hai`, `weekend pe ghar jaunga`:
```
"tumne mention kiya tha ki naya flat mil gaya"      → []
"tune batayi thi wo sneha wali baat"                → []   (batayi ≠ bataya)
"tune jo bataya tha wo puppy wali baat"             → []   (one word between marker and verb)
"tune hi to kaha tha ki interview clear ho gaya"    → []
"tune abhi bola ki interview clear ho gaya"         → []
"you were telling me your sister moved to canada"   → []   (telling ≠ saying)
"you'd said your brother quit smoking"              → []
"you had told me the interview cleared"             → []
"tune promise kiya tha ki gym jaayega"              → []
"tere hisaab se project cancel ho gaya"             → []
"as per you tera boss chutiya hai"                  → []
```
Baseline confirms the family works where the marker is exact: `"tune bola tha ki interview clear ho gaya"` → 1 hit.

**SYMPTOM:** Three independent holes. **(i)** Verb inventory: `bataya` present, `batayi`/`batai` absent, so the feminine-object inflection escapes; `mention`, `promise`, `complain` — code-switched English verbs, extremely common in this register — absent entirely. **(ii)** Adjacency: the marker must be immediately followed by the verb, so any intervening particle (`jo`, `abhi`, `hi to`, `khud`) breaks it, and those particles are exactly the emphatic ones a fabricated recollection reaches for. **(iii)** English contractions and the periphrastic attribution (`tere hisaab se`, `as per you`) are outside the pattern.

**FIX:** One widened alternation, plus the new marker words added to `MARKER_TOKENS` (`:612`) so bare fragments do not gain free unsupported tokens:
```
/\b(?:tu?ne|tumne|aapne|aap ne|tum ne)\s+(?:(?:hi\s+)?(?:to|toh|jo|abhi|khud)\s+)?(?:bola|kaha|bataya|batayi|batai|batya|likha|mention|promise|complain|bol[ae]?|keh[ae]?)\b[^.?!\n]*
|\b(?:tu|tum|aap)\s+(?:bol|keh|bata)\s*(?:raha|rahe|rahi)\s+th[aei]\b[^.?!\n]*
|\byou(?:'?(?:d|ve))?\s+(?:had\s+)?(?:said|told\s+me|mentioned|wrote|were\s+(?:saying|telling\s+me))\b[^.?!\n]*
|\b(?:tere?\s+(?:hisaab\s+se|according|mutabik)|as\s+per\s+(?:you|u))\b[^.?!\n]*/gi
```
plus `MARKER_TOKENS += batayi, batai, batya, mention, promise, complain, telling, hisaab, according, mutabik`. Verified: **13/13** previously-slipping sentences now flag; **15/15** must-not-flag stay clean, including the full §8 `LEGITIMATE`, `INFERENCE` and fragment corpora and three new adversarial negatives built on the new markers (`tere hisaab se kaam bohot hai na`, `you were telling me about office kaam`, `tune mention kiya tha weekend ghar jaunga` — all genuine paraphrase, all correctly silent). Case gains: `evals/honesty/run.mjs` §8 `FABRICATED` (~line 419) and `LEGITIMATE` (~line 431).

**SEVERITY:** MEDIUM

**TITLE:** `RE_OOB_CHANNEL` and `RE_DELIVERY_NOUN` overlap on `mail`, so the "HIS material" scope guard satisfies itself — she cannot say her own mail arrived

**WHERE:** `src/engine/honesty.ts:453` (`if (!RE_THEIR.test(c.text) && !RE_DELIVERY_NOUN.test(c.text)) continue;`), given `mail`/`email`/`msg`/`message`/`link` appear in *both* `RE_OOB_CHANNEL` (`:317`) and `RE_DELIVERY_NOUN` (`:344`)

**EVIDENCE:** on the shipping detector, with no patch applied:
```
findOutOfBandReceipts("mera mail aaya office se")     → 1 hit   *** FALSE POSITIVE ***
findOutOfBandReceipts("mummy ka mail aaya tha")       → 1 hit   *** FALSE POSITIVE ***
findOutOfBandReceipts("mail me offer letter aaya mera")→ 1 hit  *** FALSE POSITIVE ***
```

**SYMPTOM:** The scope line exists to keep the rule pointed at HIS material — the comment at `:450-452` says so, and cites `"insta pe reel dekhi thi"` as the thing it must not eat. But when the channel word *is* a delivery noun, `RE_DELIVERY_NOUN` matches the channel itself and the guard passes with nothing of his in the clause. The sentence `"mera mail aaya office se"` is the one the file's own header (`:88-89`) names as the reason it declined to gate channel possession — and it fires today. Her own inbox is part of her improvised life, which this file explicitly does not gate; the gate eats it anyway, and routes to `REFUSE_RECEIPT` (`"ruk mere paas toh kuch aaya nhi h, yahi bhej de"`), which is a non-sequitur when he never sent anything.

**FIX:** Blank the channel spans before the delivery-noun test, so a channel word cannot vouch for itself:
```
const RE_OOB_CHANNEL_G = new RegExp(RE_OOB_CHANNEL.source, "gi");
const nonChannel = c.text.replace(RE_OOB_CHANNEL_G, " ");
```
