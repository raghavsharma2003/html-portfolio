Our research asks whether the engine can change without breaking the identity above it.
The parts only matter if they hold together.
Identity, memory, culture, and expression are not separate product features. They are one system viewed from different moments.
Identity continuity
How can values, boundaries, temperament, and recognizable behavior hold across conversations and model changes?
Relational memory
How should an AI remember what mattered, preserve uncertainty, accept correction, and forget deliberately?
Social presence
How can voice, interruption, timing, gaze, and reaction feel like expressions of one coherent state?
Cultural intelligence
How can an AI understand code-switching, intimacy, hierarchy, humor, and family context beyond literal translation?

Feeling human is not a metric.
The difficult failures appear across time. We intend to evaluate continuity where a one-minute demo cannot hide the seams.
One turn
Did the timing, tone, and response fit the moment?
One month
Did memory preserve meaning without inventing a past?
Model change

Where did the identity drift?
What did the relationship change?
Human presence demands clearer boundaries, not weaker ones.
Trust is part of the architecture. The system should make its identity, memory, uncertainty, and permissions legible.
Always AI
Meera should never impersonate a person or hide what she is. Presence does not require deception.
Memory you control
What is remembered should be inspectable, correctable, exportable, and erasable by the user.
Connection without capture
No guilt, coercion, or dependence mechanics designed to monopolize attention.
Honest uncertainty
When the system does not know, cannot remember, or may be wrong, it should say so plainly.
