The research behind it
The companion we are building.
Conversation that breathes
We are building voice around turn prediction, interruption and comfortable silence, so talking can feel fluid instead of request and response.
A shared history
The aim is memory with judgement: what mattered can shape the next conversation, without turning every personal detail into permanent storage.
Context she can see
When you choose to share a view, an object or an expression, vision should become part of the same conversation rather than a separate feature.
Expression that agrees
Voice, words, timing, gaze and facial response should express one underlying state, without the seams between models showing through.
A recognisable identity
A point of view, tastes, humour, boundaries and contradictions that remain coherent while leaving room for genuine change.
Clearly AI
Presence should not depend on deception. Meera will identify as AI and communicate uncertainty, memory limits and perceptual boundaries plainly.
This is early work. We will say so.
A carefully edited minute can make almost any companion look complete. Identity drift, false memories and repetitive behaviour only appear with time. That is the real test.
We will describe what Meera can perceive, remember and do, publish meaningful limitations, and expand access as the experience earns it.
Request early access.
When access begins, we will invite people gradually and learn from long-term use. Tell us who you are and what you hope a companion could become.
