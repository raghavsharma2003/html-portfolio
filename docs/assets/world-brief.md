# World paintings — GPT Image prompt pack (the sky that follows the clock)

One consistent style across ALL images. Generate the night one first,
then feed it back as the style reference for the other four.

## Style block (paste into every prompt)
> Soft painterly digital illustration, gentle Ghibli-adjacent warmth,
> wide dreamy sky over a distant Indian city skyline (water tanks,
> low apartment blocks, a bridge over a river, faraway hills), tiny
> warm window lights, no people, no text, no logos, no watermark,
> clean gradient sky occupying the top two-thirds, city small at the
> bottom third, muted cinematic color, high detail but calm.

## The five states (portrait 1080x1920 each, PLUS a wide 1920x1080 crop)
1. `world_night.jpg` — deep indigo night, crescent moon upper left,
   sparse bright stars, thin silver-edged clouds, city lights golden.
2. `world_predawn.jpg` — the hour before sunrise: ink blue fading to a
   thin peach line at the horizon, morning star, city mostly asleep.
3. `world_morning.jpg` — soft warm morning, pale gold light, a few
   small white clouds, kite or two far away, city waking.
4. `world_golden.jpg` — late golden afternoon, honeyed light, long soft
   clouds, warm haze over the city.
5. `world_dusk.jpg` — just after sunset: rose-to-violet gradient, first
   stars appearing, city lights switching on, one long lavender cloud.

## Parallax extras (transparent PNG)
6. `cloud_a.png`, `cloud_b.png` — two loose soft clouds matching the
   night palette, isolated on transparency, for the drifting layer.

## Delivery
Largest size offered, exact filenames, one folder — same handoff as the
photo pack. The app ships with procedural skies immediately; your
paintings swap in via one variable per state, zero code change.
