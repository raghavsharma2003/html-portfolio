Delete your Maya account

This removes your account and everything Maya knows about you. It cannot be undone.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in identity (email, phone, Google link).
  • Every conversation, call record, photo and file you shared.
  • Everything Maya remembers about you: facts, episodes, patterns, milestones.
  • Synced app state across your devices.

Deletion runs across every data table immediately; encrypted backups roll off within 30 days. Nothing is retained for advertising. There are no ads.

Delete from the app (fastest)

Open Maya → the … menu → Make her forget you. Confirming runs the full deletion above, instantly.

Delete from the web

Sign in at meera-silk.vercel.app/chat with the same account, open the … menu and choose Make her forget you. Works without the app installed.

Can't sign in?

Email compliance@carbonsettle.com from the address on your account (or include your account phone number) and we'll run the deletion for you within 7 days, with a confirmation reply.

This is permanent. If you only want a fresh start, the app's Clear chat keeps your account but empties the conversation instead. See also our privacy policy.
