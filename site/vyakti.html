What you get

Not a voice clone. A person your students already know.

Voice is the part everyone demos. It is also the part that wears off in about a week if there is nothing behind it. What makes a student come back is being remembered.

It remembers each student separately.

Not a chat history. A per-student record: what they keep getting wrong, what they were dreading before the last mock, what finally clicked. Rushing integration by parts is stored as a pattern with the two sessions that evidenced it, and your clone can cite them.

It stays current from your own channel.

Link your channel and a new video becomes a set of proposed updates to how your clone teaches. Proposed, always. Nothing changes what your clone says to a student until you have read the change and tapped accept.

It teaches the way you teach.

Strictness and warmth are separate settings, because they are separate in real teachers. Your doubt-handling ladder decides what a student gets before they get a full solution, which is also how the product refuses to be a homework machine.

It is disclosed, every time.

A card before the first turn and a spoken line at the top of every call: this is an AI clone, built from your recordings, and it is not you. You see the exact wording in the studio before you publish. It cannot be shortened or switched off, including by us.

Every second of audio is signed.

Watermarked twice and signed with C2PA provenance. If a clip of your cloned voice turns up somewhere it should not be, it is identifiable as synthetic and traceable to the generation that made it.

Withdrawal actually deletes.

Revoking stops future use in the same transaction that invalidates the voice profile, then queues provider copies and private storage for erasure with durable retries. The studio shows you each one being confirmed, not a message saying it was requested.

The path

Nine steps, and you can see all nine from the first one.

The studio never hides what is still locked or who it is waiting on. Every step below says whether it is your turn or ours.

  1. 01

    Name your clone

    A workspace, and nothing else yet. Your turn. About a minute.

  2. 02

    Give source permission

    Four statements you confirm yourself, recorded as a receipt you can withdraw. This permits us to receive, transcribe and store what you send. It permits nothing else. Your turn.

  3. 03

    Add one recording

    One lecture, one voice note, one PDF of your notes. One is enough to see a draft. The file is fingerprinted in your browser before it moves, and the filename is never stored. Your turn.

  4. 04

    Prove you are you

    A government ID and a live challenge you record on the spot. This is the step that makes cloning someone else structurally impossible rather than against the rules. The raw ID and the challenge media are erased once the match is bound. Your turn.

  5. 05

    Review the sheet we drafted

    Subject, syllabus coverage, strictness, warmth, the doubt ladder, the mistakes you see most. Drafted from your uploads with every claim cited to the moment it came from, and approved by you one at a time. Your turn.

  6. 06

    Hear it

    A private preview that cannot join a call or activate anything. Then a blind A and B lab where you pick which of two candidates is closer, without being told which is which, because you cannot judge your own voice once you know the answer. Your turn, and the part teachers enjoy.

  7. 07

    Talk to it

    A calibration call with your own clone. It listens to how you actually sound and how you actually phrase things, and proposes changes while you are talking. You accept or ignore each one. Nothing lands on your sheet without a tap. Your turn.

  8. 08

    We run the gates

    Identity, intelligibility, artifacts, watermark, privacy and latency checks, plus the fidelity score against your ceiling. If it does not clear, we refuse to activate and tell you which check failed. Ours. Nothing you can do to speed it up, and we will not pretend otherwise.

  9. 09

    Choose where it can be reached

    A web link, an embed on your own site, or a messaging channel. You see the student's disclosure card before you choose any of them, because that is the informed half of informed consent. Your turn.

Non-negotiable

The rules your clone cannot be talked out of.

These are not settings, and they are not instructions given to a model in a prompt. They are checks on the way out, which is the difference between a preference and a guarantee. Most of your students are sixteen or seventeen years old, and that is the reason.

  • ✓ Your clone never denies being an AI, and never implies you read or saw anything a student said.
  • ✓ Crisis helplines are guaranteed present, verified by a test that fails the build if they go missing.
  • ✓ Every student account defaults to the minor-safe tier. Engagement mechanics are structurally off, not merely disabled.
  • ✓ No streak anxiety, no manufactured urgency, no countdown to the exam. A mechanic ships only if removing every fear from it leaves it intact.
  • ✓ Your clone never speaks first without a reason it can cite. Silence is not a reason.
  • ✓ A full solution is never the first answer to a doubt. Your ladder decides what comes before it.

Read this before you sign up

What we have not proven yet.

  • No one has run a blind listening test. 0.7753 is embedding similarity, which is a real measurement of a real thing and is not the same thing as sounding like you. The test rig exists. Until a person has sat through it, we will not tell you how it sounds.
  • We have not benchmarked ourselves against ElevenLabs or anyone else. When we do, the result goes here whichever way it comes out.
  • The first clone off a cold start takes about three minutes because the voice runtime scales to zero. You will see the real wait, not a progress bar that finishes early.
  • Deployment channels are built and not yet turned on. The web link and the embed come first, messaging platforms after.
