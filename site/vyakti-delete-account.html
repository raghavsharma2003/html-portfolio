Owner control

Stop a clone and erase its data.

Revoking a clone disables future use immediately. Deletion of private storage and provider copies then continues through a verified erasure job.

Revoke a clone in the studio

Sign in, select the clone, open its owner controls, and choose Revoke clone. Confirming blocks generation and queues sources, derived models, memories, channel access, and provider copies for erasure. The studio keeps showing the verified progress.

Open the studio

Delete one source

In Feed, open the source list and choose Delete for the recording, video, document, or image. The source is disabled before the storage erasure completes. Removing a source may invalidate a draft or active capability that depended on it.

Delete the account identity too

The current self-service control revokes and erases clone data. For deletion of the sign-in account itself, email compliance@carbonsettle.com from the address used to sign in. Include only the account email and the request. Do not attach identity documents or recordings.

If you cannot sign in

Email the same address from the account email. We may need a narrow ownership check before deleting data so another person cannot erase your clone. We will not ask you to email a government ID or raw biometric recording.

What may remain temporarily

Encrypted backups can expire after the live copy is deleted. Security records and a content-free deletion receipt may be retained where needed to prevent abuse, meet legal duties, or prove that erasure completed. They are not used to operate a clone.

This action is permanent. A revoked clone cannot generate new output. Recreating it requires new source permissions and must pass the applicable gates again.
